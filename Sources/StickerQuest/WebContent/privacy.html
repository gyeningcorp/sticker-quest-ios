⭐ Privacy Policy – Sticker Quest

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Sticker Quest is a chore and behavior chart app for children ages 4 and up, designed to be used by parents together with their kids. This policy explains, in plain language, how the app handles information.

Sticker Quest works entirely on your device. There is no account, no login, and no server. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your information. Nothing you enter ever leaves your device.

No Accounts

Sticker Quest does not require — or offer — an account. You tap Start Playing and use the app immediately. There is no sign-in, no email, and no password.

What Information We Collect

None. We do not collect any personal information from parents or children. All app data — such as a child's name, avatar, chores, points, levels, unlocked stickers, and streaks — is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email address, or contact information
  • No account or login identifiers
  • No location data
  • No microphone or camera access
  • No behavioral profiling or fingerprinting
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA or similar)
  • No third-party analytics SDKs
  • No in-app chat or social features
  • No user-generated content uploads

Where Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device only and never leaves it. Sticker Quest has no backend server and makes no external network connections.

Syncing Across Devices

If you want to move your progress to another device, the app lets you generate an export code that you enter on the other device. This transfer is initiated by you, on your devices — it does not route through any server we operate, and we never see your data.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Sticker Quest is designed for use by children ages 4+ under direct parental supervision and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • No personal information is collected from children or parents
  • No data is transmitted off the device
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties
  • No advertising or behavioral tracking of any kind
  • No third-party analytics SDKs
  • No location tracking
  • No social or chat features

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone, because we do not collect or receive any data in the first place.

Data Deletion

Because all data lives only on your device, you are always in full control. To erase it, go to Grown-Ups → Settings → Reset Data, or simply delete the app.

In-App Purchases

Premium features are available as a one-time purchase through Apple In-App Purchases. No payment information is collected or stored by this app. All billing is handled entirely by Apple.

No Advertising

Sticker Quest contains zero advertisements. There are no ad networks, no behavioral tracking, and no advertising SDKs of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
